# Experiment 019: full-vocabulary homophonic channel

18 October 2026. Design before calibration generation, optimization or evaluation. Previous turn made verified progress: release0.18 tested novel collapsed patterns and passed CI. Read selected sections2.2/3.1 of Ravi and Knight (2011), ACL P11-1025, for homophonic/noisy-channel and type-level updates. Our deterministic surjective key search is an independent simpler method, not their Bayesian CRP/Gibbs/word-dictionary algorithm or a replication of Zodiac results.

Use all training sign types in the complete637-ID ICIT benchmark, rather than only32 frequent types. All IDs remain distinct. Only source types absent from training map to a fixed UNK source slot and target unit32. Every training glyph maps to exactly one of the32 selected Tamil/Vedic writing units; multiple glyphs may share a unit. This is a conditional homophonic hypothesis, not a demonstrated property of Indus or a historically validated sound inventory. Target normalization,32-unit counts and chronology/genre limitations remain from Experiment017. It does not acquire every historical sign or reconstruct missing target-language units.

Use near_copy.components: exact/reverse groups plus transitive one-substitution neighbors at equal lengths>=3. Shuffle components seed42, assign80/10/10 percent of components; disclose row imbalance and largest component. These are similarity groups, not verified archaeological families. Choose the entire source vocabulary from training only. Preserve every token, including unseen slots. Report source coverage and fixed-UNK collapsed-pattern crossings without retrospective removal.

Fit a normalized glyph-generating channel. For a proposed key g, each target unit must have at least one assigned training glyph (surjection). Its emission distribution is P(s|g(s))=(f_s+0.5)/sum_{r:g(r)=g(s)}(f_r+0.5), using training token frequencies only; fixed source UNK emits with probability1 from target UNK. Target initial-unit unigram and internal bigrams use the frozen add0.5/strength20 model, no EOS. A string probability multiplies target likelihood and every glyph emission. Report total glyph-space bits/token, plus target/emission parts. This emission cost is necessary: optimizing only recoded-string fluency could reward merging most signs without explaining their distinct glyphs. Enforce nonempty groups so the model normalizes over the declared source alphabet.

Five starts per condition: source-frequency order cyclically assigned to target ranks, plus four random surjections seeds17/29/43/59. Each gets30,000 proposals, half random single-type moves (reject if emptying a unit), half random type swaps, geometric temperature20→0.05 total bits, rounded1e-8 deltas; keep best training key. Then up to two sequential greedy single-type sweeps over all32 target choices. Validation chooses restart only; test follows selection. Export all keys/validation scores, selected test per-text losses, group memberships, coverage, unit occupancy, restart agreement, first20 explicitly hypothetical recodings and baselines. Include a fixed cyclic-key baseline, and training-only source unigram plus strength20 sign bigram (same initial/internal, no EOS) as generic structural predictors. No test-driven hyperparameter or direction selection; use stored source order and disclose that uncertainty.

First calibrate on a fresh known two-homophones-per-unit artificial cipher. Recover plaintext units from the frozen Experiment017 synthetic calibration input, then choose one of two opaque glyph aliases independently per token (seed2031), scrambling64 aliases seed1031. Keep rare target32 as the fixed UNK marker. Fit using the same similarity-component split and search budget. Truth is used only to construct the cipher and after validation selection to measure known-source key/token accuracy; never passed to optimizer. Report whether the true key is surjective on observed training glyphs; if not, the oracle lies outside the constrained space and calibration is limited. Calibration windows may overlap within their held source documents, which never trained the target model.

Then execute Indus and the explicitly nonphonetic seed17 administrative control for each proxy. No known-key accuracy exists for real Indus. Gain over generic unigram or arbitrary cyclic assignment, even with positive calibration, cannot establish a language or translation; compare the nonphonetic control and report failures. All conditional keys remain unadopted. Full decipherment and exhaustive acquisition remain open.
