17 October 2026. Acquisition design recorded after inspecting the first/last appendix pages and the postscript, before complete transcription or automated audit. The book's introduction describes 179 variant-bearing base signs and 641 drawn forms; its postscript adds two base signs, 418/419, and a separate variant-addendum column. Do not assume the prose totals include that later column.

Keep the original PDF hash/page/column and source order. Count main and addendum forms/families separately, compare with source totals and investigate disagreement without editing counts to fit.

Variant forms may include the normal form and do not represent hundreds of additional independent signs or sound values.

Audit cited examples against all frozen primary application rows, preserving their original line/face/uncertainty. A candidate occurrence must contain the cited base ID. Preserve parenthetical suffixes without guessing their meaning; do not assign a variant to every repeated base occurrence. Source family membership is attributed to the original editor, not independently proven phonetic equivalence.
