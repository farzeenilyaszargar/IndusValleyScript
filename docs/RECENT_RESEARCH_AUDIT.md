## SIGIL preprint

Nikhil Raghavendra, [On the Non-Specificity of Statistical Measures Used in Script Decipherment](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02999v1), arXiv:2608.02999v1, 4 August 2026. Inspected construction, evaluation, prediction, lexical-stress, discussion and limitations passages.
