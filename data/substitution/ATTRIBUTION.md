# Attributed substitution-model inputs

The Tamil portions of inputs.json.gz derive from the pinned Sentamizh Corpus (indic-corpora; Revathi Prasad and Vijayalakshmi Prasad; selected Project Madurai-based original-text fields) under Apache-2.0. The Vedic portions derive from UD Sanskrit-Vedic (Oliver Hellwig, Salvatore Scarlata, Elia Ackermann, Paul Widmer, Erica Biagetti, Sven Sellmer and upstream contributors) under CC BY-SA4.0. The adapted Vedic numeric data remain CC BY-SA4.0. Source revisions, hashes and edition limitations are in docs/language_resource_inventory.json and docs/LANGUAGE_RESOURCES.md. License texts and full earlier attribution are retained in ../language_samples/TAMIL_LICENSE.txt, VEDIC_LICENSE.txt and ATTRIBUTION.md.

Changes: existing NFC/NFD letter-plus-mark normalization, document-group partitioning, top32 writing-unit selection, rare-unit collapse retaining slots, unigram/bigram counts, short-window sampling and a known artificial substitution. Selected single writing-unit Unicode labels are included; word dictionaries, original complete texts, translations and linguistic annotations are omitted. These labels are not reconstructed Indus phonemes or accepted sound values. Preparation: scripts/prepare_substitution.py, requiring hash-verified local source files. Offline search: scripts/substitution_search.py.

Experiment 019 reuses these frozen inputs and generates an artificial two-alias cipher per selected writing unit; glyph aliases, split memberships and recovery diagnostics in results/homophonic_channel.json are derived numeric data. Vedic-derived adapted calibration data retain CC BY-SA4.0 with the same upstream credit and license text. Source frequencies and selected Unicode labels are unchanged; generated aliases are not original ancient glyphs or translations.
